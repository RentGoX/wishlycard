The problem

Eligible donors are often minutes away — but no one knows to ask them.

Blood banks across India regularly run short of specific groups, especially during emergencies, festivals, and in smaller towns where donor networks are informal or word-of-mouth. Families end up calling relatives, posting on social media, and hoping someone with the right blood group sees it in time.

Meanwhile, willing donors exist nearby — they just aren't reachable at the right moment. RaktSaathi closes that gap with automatic, location-aware matching the instant a request is made.

Rare groups (O−, AB−) are the hardest to find on short notice.
In a critical case, every extra hour of searching adds real risk.
Donors want to help, but rarely know exactly when they're needed.

How it works

Five steps, mostly automatic

From the moment a request is created to a conversation with a donor — the matching itself needs no manual work.

01

Request is created

Patient's name, blood group, units needed, hospital, and urgency level — hospital location and contact details are filled in automatically from GPS and the requester's profile.

02

Compatible donors are found

The system checks blood-group compatibility and searches outward in radius tiers — starting close by and expanding automatically until enough eligible donors are found.

03

Donors are notified instantly

A push notification goes out with the blood group, distance, and hospital — nothing more. No donor's exact address is ever shown to anyone.

04

A donor accepts

The first donors to respond are confirmed — once enough donors have accepted for the units needed, the request closes automatically so no one is over-notified.

05

Requester and donor connect

A private chat opens between the two so they can coordinate — final confirmation always happens at a licensed hospital or blood bank.

What's inside

Everything an emergency donor network needs

Built as a complete, production-grade system — not a prototype.

Smart, privacy-first matching

Distance is calculated server-side using real geographic indexing — donors see requests near them, but their exact home location is never exposed to anyone, ever.

Verified donors, honestly labelled

Every donor starts as "self-declared." Admins review uploaded documents before a blood group is ever shown as verified — and the app never makes a medical decision on its own.

Three urgency levels

Normal, urgent, and critical requests are prioritised so genuine emergencies stand out.

Donor dashboard

Availability toggle, donation history, and incoming requests — all in one place for donors.

Built-in secure chat

Once a donor accepts, a private thread opens automatically — no phone numbers exchanged upfront.

Full admin panel

Verification review, fraud reports, hospital management, and audit logs — all governed by the same access rules as the app itself.

Privacy & safety

Designed so trust isn't optional

Every rule below is enforced at the database level — not just hidden in the interface. A modified app can't bypass them.

Blood availability is never guaranteed by the app. Final confirmation — blood grouping, screening, and cross-matching — always happens through a licensed blood bank or hospital.

No exact location, ever

Only a rounded distance is shown to anyone other than the donor themselves.

No blood marketplace

No pricing, bidding, or blood-selling of any kind — this is a community matching tool only.

Documents stay private

Verification uploads are stored in a private, access-controlled bucket — visible only to the donor and admins.

Abuse protection built in

Rate limits on requests and acceptances, spam flagging, and a reporting system for fraudulent requests.
